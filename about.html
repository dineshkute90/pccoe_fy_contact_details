About Team Mathematics, PCCOE

The Department of Applied Sciences & Humanities at PCCOE houses a vibrant team of mathematicians dedicated to fostering analytical thinking and research culture.

Our faculty members have published over 30 research papers in reputed international journals and have guided numerous students to success in national-level mathematical competitions. We believe in bridging the gap between theoretical mathematics and engineering applications.

Our Vision

To become a center of excellence in mathematical education, research, and collaborative problem-solving, producing engineers who can tackle complex real-world challenges.

Core Values

  • Innovation in pedagogy
  • Interdisciplinary research
  • Student-centric mentorship
  • Ethical academic practices
